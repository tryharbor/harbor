Your first clients don't need to find you through a perfect website. They need to hear about you from someone they trust, or find you when they search "house cleaning near me". Here's a plan for the first four weeks.

## Week 1: tell everyone you know

Send a short, personal message to friends, family, neighbors and old coworkers. Don't ask them to hire you; ask them to pass it on.

> Hi Sam! I've started a house cleaning business in [your area]. If you or anyone you know needs a hand, I'd love the work. First clean is [$ off / an extra room free]. Here's my number: [number].

Offer a small first-clean discount, not a permanent low price. Every person who books is a chance for a referral and a review.

## Week 1: set up your Google Business Profile

This is free, and it's how most people searching "cleaners near me" will find you.

- **Hide your home address.** Google lets service businesses that visit customers remove their address and show the area they serve instead.
- **List your service areas.** You can list up to 20, and Google asks you to keep them within about two hours' drive of where you're based.
- **Add photos of your work and a clear list of services:** standard clean, deep clean, move-out clean.
- **Ask for reviews.** Ask every client to leave one, with a direct link or a QR code. Google doesn't allow discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews, so keep reviews and offers separate.

## Week 2: be seen on the streets you want

Pick the neighborhoods you'd like to work in, ideally close together, so you spend less time driving.

- **Door hangers or flyers** on the streets you want. Repetition works: the same street a few times over a few weeks.
- **Yard signs** (from about $11 each at Vistaprint). Ask a happy client if you can leave one up for a week after a clean.
- **Business cards** to leave with every client (100 for about $15).

## Week 3: try a small local ad

Nextdoor lets you run ads for your neighborhood for under $5 a day. Keep the ad simple: what you do, where, and one reason to choose you ("insured, flexible weekday times, same cleaner every visit"). Run it for two weeks and count the messages it brings.

## Week 4: turn first cleans into regulars

The goal isn't ten one-off cleans; it's ten clients who book you every week or two.

- **At the end of every first clean**, offer a regular slot: "I have Thursday mornings open every other week. Want me to hold it for you?"
- **Ask for a referral:** "If you know anyone who'd like the same, I'd be grateful."
- **Ask for a Google review** with a direct link, sent the same day.

## Other places worth a try

- **Real estate agents and property managers** often need move-out cleans at short notice. Introduce yourself with your prices and availability.
- **Local Facebook groups:** many neighborhoods have one. Read the rules first; some only allow business posts on certain days.

## Google's rules worth knowing

- **Use your real business name** on your Google Business Profile. Google doesn't allow extra service words in the name ("Sparkle Cleaning Services Best Maids Austin"), and profiles that add them can be suspended.
- **Ask for reviews, never pay for them.** Google encourages asking customers to leave a review through a link or QR code, but offering discounts, gifts or free services in exchange for reviews is strictly prohibited. Keep your first-visit offers and your review requests separate.

## FAQ

### Do I need a website to get my first clients?

Not to get the first few: referrals and your Google Business Profile do most of the work. A simple website helps once people start searching for your business name, because it shows your services, prices and areas and lets them get in touch.

### Should I lower my prices to win clients?

Use a first-clean offer instead. A permanently low price is hard to raise later, and the clients it attracts often leave when you do.

### How many clients do I need to be full time?

Work it out from your schedule. For example, two cleans a day, five days a week, every other week per client works out to 20 recurring clients.
