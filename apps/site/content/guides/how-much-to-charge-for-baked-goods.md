Home bakers most often underprice because they count the butter and sugar but not the hours. Decorating, packaging and delivery are where the time goes. Count all of it.

## Work out your own price

**Price = ingredients + packaging + (your hourly pay × hours), then add room for card fees.**

An example: a dozen decorated sugar cookies, with numbers to swap for your own.

- **Butter, flour, sugar and an egg** at October 2026 prices (BLS): half a pound of butter ($1.99), 1¼ pounds of flour (67¢), half a pound of sugar (50¢) and one egg (19¢): $3.35.
- **Powdered sugar, meringue powder and colors** for the icing, say **$2**: $2.
- **A windowed cookie box** (36¢) and a **label** (20¢): 56¢.
- **2 hours** of baking, icing and packing at **$30 an hour**: $60. For context, the median pay for bakers was $17.86 an hour in May 2025 (BLS).
- Subtotal: $65.91.
- Card fees (Stripe, 2.9% + 30¢ online): the price needs to be about **$68**.

Ingredients are less than a tenth of that price. Your time is the real cost, so price decorated and custom work by the hours it takes.

## What supermarkets charge

Supermarket prices aren't your competition, but they're a useful floor. In October 2026, the U.S. average for **chocolate chip cookies** was $5.25 a pound and for **white bread** $1.86 a pound (BLS). Home-baked, custom and decorated goods sell well above that, because customers are paying for fresh, handmade work.

## Wedding cakes

- **The Knot:** couples married in 2025 paid an average of **$540** for their wedding cake (2026 Real Weddings Study, 10,474 couples).
- **Zola:** wedding cake and desserts together average **$917**, with most couples paying $700–$1,100 (2026).
- **Thumbtack:** wedding cakes booked there average $189–$409, by guest count:

| Servings | Average price |

Price wedding and tiered cakes by the serving, so a bigger cake costs more in a way customers understand.

## Custom orders and deposits

- **Set a minimum order** for custom work, so a small order still pays for your setup and cleanup.
- **Take a deposit** when someone books a date, so a cancellation doesn't leave you with an empty weekend.
- **List add-ons with prices:** extra tiers, fondant, sugar flowers, printed images, delivery.

## Remember the sales cap and the taxman

Many states cap yearly cottage food sales, at $35,000 in Washington, $50,000 in New Jersey and Michigan, and $150,000 in Texas. Pricing properly means you earn more before you reach it. Self-employment tax is **15.3%** of your net earnings, on top of income tax.

Baked goods are often exempt from sales tax as food, but not always: in Texas, bakery items are taxable from a seller that isn't a bakery when sold with eating utensils, and in Pennsylvania cookies sold at fairs are taxable. Check your state's revenue department.

## FAQ

### How much should I charge for a dozen cookies?

Work it out from your costs. In the example above, a dozen decorated sugar cookies needed about $68, mostly for two hours of work. Plain drop cookies take far less time, so they cost less.

### How much should I charge for a custom cake?

Price it by servings and the time the decoration takes, using the formula above. For wedding cakes, couples paid an average of $540 (The Knot, 2026), and Thumbtack's average for a cake serving 30 or fewer is $230.

### Should I charge less because I bake from home?

No. Customers pay for fresh, handmade baking, and your time costs the same wherever you bake. Charge enough to pay yourself properly.
